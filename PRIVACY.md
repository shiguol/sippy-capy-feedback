# 喝水卡皮 隐私政策 / Sippy Capy Privacy Policy

[English](#english) | 中文

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

本政策适用于「喝水卡皮」Chrome 扩展和 Android 应用（包名 `com.happymoon.sippycapy`），开发者为 Happy Moon Studio。

## 我们收集什么

**什么都不收集。** 喝水卡皮没有服务器、没有账号系统，也不包含任何统计、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。

- 你的提醒设置（间隔、工作时段、每日目标、杯量）和每一次饮水记录，**只保存在你自己的设备上**。
- Android 应用连网络权限都没有申请，不会把数据发送到任何地方。它只用到：
  - **通知**：显示喝水提醒和每日达标通知；
  - **开机启动广播**：手机重启后把提醒重新排好。
- 如果你开启了 Android 系统备份，设置和饮水记录会随系统备份保存到**你自己的** Google 账号里，开发者无法访问。Chrome 扩展的设置通过 Chrome 自带的同步在你的设备之间同步，同样只在你的 Google 账号内。

## 导出数据

你可以在应用的「统计」页把全部记录导出为 CSV 或 JSON 文件。文件保存在你选择的位置，是否分享由你决定。

## 删除数据

卸载应用（或在系统设置里「清除存储空间」）即可删除全部数据。

## 反馈

应用里的「反馈」按钮会打开本 GitHub 仓库的公开页面。链接里只带应用版本号和系统版本，不带任何饮水记录。你在 GitHub 上提交的内容所有人可见，并受 GitHub 隐私政策约束。

## 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的个人信息。

## 变更与联系

政策如有变更会更新本页面并修改生效日期。有问题请在本仓库[提交 Issue](https://github.com/shiguol/sippy-capy-feedback/issues/new)。

## English

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy covers the Sippy Capy Chrome extension and the Android app (package `com.happymoon.sippycapy`), developed by Happy Moon Studio.

### What we collect

**Nothing.** Sippy Capy has no servers and no accounts, and it includes no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

- Your reminder settings (interval, work hours, daily goal, glass sizes) and every drink you log are **stored only on your own device**.
- The Android app does not even request network access, and sends no data anywhere. The only permissions it uses are:
  - **Notifications**, to show drink reminders and the daily-goal notification;
  - **Run at startup**, to reschedule reminders after your phone restarts.
- If Android system backup is turned on, your settings and records are included in the backup stored in **your own** Google account; the developer cannot access it. The Chrome extension's settings sync between your devices through Chrome's built-in sync, also within your own Google account.

### Exporting your data

You can export all your records as a CSV or JSON file from the Stats screen. The file is saved where you choose, and it's up to you whether to share it.

### Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in system settings) deletes all of its data.

### Feedback

The feedback buttons in the app open public pages in this GitHub repository. The links include only the app version and OS version, never your drinking records. Anything you post on GitHub is public and subject to GitHub's privacy policy.

### Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

### Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. Questions? [Open an issue](https://github.com/shiguol/sippy-capy-feedback/issues/new) in this repository.
